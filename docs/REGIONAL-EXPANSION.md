# Regional neighborhoods

Version 1.2.0 adds 80 shops and 120 six-beat scenes. Every region has 20 businesses; Kanto retains its 50 scenes and each later region now has 25. Every new store has a distinct generation-native Pokémon manager, authentic PMD portrait, local body sprite, item artwork, upgrade and income loop.

New scenes play automatically as their milestones are earned. Later regions require 20 shops, 10 managers, all 25 unlocked scenes and their existing earnings goal before travel. Returning to town holds the currently available scene queue for that session; future milestones still play. The journal supports replay and an explicit autoplay preference.

Old saves retain their existing 100 businesses and receive 80 empty slots. The ten original scene IDs per later region stay stable. The fifteen inserted scenes come before the old farewell; an already watched farewell stays watched. Previously unlocked regions stay unlocked.

## Editing the expansion

`src/regional-stores.js` defines the new shops. `src/catalog.js` maps them into economic tiers without changing the original prices or order. `src/regional-story-expansion.js` contains the new dialogue and chapter descriptions; `src/regional-story.js` assigns stable scene IDs, regional settings and milestones. `src/story.js` preserves historical story progress when the new scenes are inserted.

No extra background images are required: each region's inspected market and landmark paths already provide grounded staging. The added conversations reuse the existing regional emotion cast; the eighty new shopkeepers appear through their manager portraits, storefronts and customer body sprites.
