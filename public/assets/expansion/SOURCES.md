# Regional neighborhood expansion artwork

Eighty additional shopkeepers use locally cached, unmodified PokeAPI pixel body sprites and 40 × 40 PMD Collab Normal portraits. Their species generation fields were verified against PokeAPI. Exact pinned portrait URLs, API endpoints and sprite sources are in [sources.json](sources.json). Each portrait folder retains its upstream credits.txt. Creator names and the CC BY-NC 4.0 terms are retained in [regional credits](../regions/SOURCES.md). Pokémon artwork rights remain with the original rights holders. All artwork is available offline.
