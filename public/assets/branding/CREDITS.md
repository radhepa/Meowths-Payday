# Meowth Mint

Meowth Mint frames the bundled Meowth Happy portrait in a mint tile. The original 40×40 portrait pixels are retained.

Portrait source: [PMD Collab](https://sprites.pmdcollab.org/), [Meowth Happy](https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab/blob/master/portrait/0052/Happy.png).
